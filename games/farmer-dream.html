🌾 Farmer Dream

Plant, water, harvest. Take your time.

How to play

  • 🌱 Drag a seed from the shop onto an empty plot.
  • 🪣 Drag the water bucket from the top bar onto a 💧 thirsty plot to start it growing. Water is free.
  • 🎃 Pumpkins need watering twice — they pause halfway.
  • ✨ Click a ripe plot to harvest it into your 🎒 inventory.
  • 🍓 Strawberries and 🍇 grapes regrow — harvest them again and again.
  • 💰 Drag from the inventory to the 💸 Sell box.
  • ⭐ Harvesting earns XP. New seeds unlock as you level up.
  • 💤 Crops keep growing while you are away, for up to four hours.
  • 🐄 Buy animals in the 🐄 Barn tab, then drag the crop they want onto them.
  • 💭 A thought bubble means hungry. One feeding makes three products.
  • 🥚 Click an animal to collect what it has made.
  • ⚙️ Drag nothing here — click a machine to load it, click again to collect.
  • 📋 Fill the order for far more than the items are worth.
  • 🔨 The Build tab sells more land, sprinklers and machines.
  • 🏡 Buy furniture, then drag it into your room. Wall things go on the wall.
  • 🐾 Pets are earned, never bought — check the album for hints.

🎉 Well farmed!
