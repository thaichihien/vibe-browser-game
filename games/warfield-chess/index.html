    📜 How to command

    This is chess — every rule is standard — but you fight it from the ground.

    • Your view is your unit's view. Pick a unit and the camera rides with it — over its shoulder, or through its eyes (⚙ Settings). Move the mouse to look around. Press Space to bring back the cursor and give orders on the field map, V to switch camera, Esc to pause. Morning mist hides everything beyond a unit's sight, and trees, rocks and bushes hide the rest.
    • Height is sight. A Rook sees over the mist from its tower deck; Pawns down in the grass see least.
    • The field map shows only your own troops. Click one of them to switch to it. Click any other square to give orders — they are checked against the rules, and a refused order tells you why (which can itself reveal what is hiding out there).
    • Click the square again or press ⚔ March to commit. Promotion is always to a Queen.
    • You hear when your troops fall and when your King is threatened — nothing more.
