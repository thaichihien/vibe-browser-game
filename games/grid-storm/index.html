📖 GRID STORM

You are the cube. One key press = one cell. Touch anything hostile, or step off the grid, and the run ends. The score is simply how long you last.

  1. 0–20s — plain missiles from the edges of a 5×5 grid. One, then two, then more.
  2. 20s — the grid tears open to 9×9 permanently, and the new edges fire too.
  3. Every 20s after — a random storm event rolls in, forever, stacking up to two at a time.
